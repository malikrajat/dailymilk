About Daily milk

Daily milk is a free, installable web app for keeping milk delivery records — milkmen, daily entries, rates, payments and reports — owned and run by one person.

By Rajat Malik · Updated 17 November 2026

What the app does

Daily milk helps a milk delivery business keep its day-to-day records and turn them into reports. It is used by the owner or operator of the business, on their own device, under their own account.

  • Milkmen — suppliers and delivery staff.
  • Daily milk entries — quantity, shift and date, so morning and evening rounds stay separate.
  • Milk rates — price lists that can change without rewriting past entries.
  • Purchases and customer payments — amounts you record for your own business; the app never moves money.
  • Reports — view on screen and export as CSV or PDF.
  • Full database export and import — one JSON file you control, restorable on another device.

Who runs it

Daily milk is an independent personal project run by Rajat Malik. It is not a company, there is no team behind it, and there is no paid tier. The app is free because it is built and maintained as a personal project, and the public guides on this site may carry advertising to cover hosting.

The operator is the person who answers the support email and writes the guides. That is unusual for a product site, and it is deliberate: there is one place to ask, and one person responsible for the answer.

Where your data is stored

The business records themselves are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) under your own account, so they are available on every device you sign in from. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, and the app never receives your Google password.

Exports are downloaded directly to your device. They are never uploaded to a shared location owned by the developer. You can export the full database as JSON at any time, and import it on another device from the Data Management settings.

The complete list of what is stored, the service providers involved and the choices you have is in the privacy policy.

Offline and installation

Daily milk is a Progressive Web App. The screens it has already loaded keep working without a connection, and entries made offline are kept on the device until the connection returns. It can be installed from the browser on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux; installing gives the most reliable offline experience.

The guides

Alongside the app, this site publishes free guides about the paperwork of a milk round: keeping daily records, calculating rates and bills, planning a route, and reducing wastage. They are written for anyone running a delivery round, whether or not they use the app.

  • How to keep daily milk delivery records
  • How to calculate milk rates and customer bills
  • Planning a milk delivery route that saves time
  • Reducing milk wastage, returns and shortages
  • Notebook, spreadsheet or app
  • Record keeping FAQ

The editorial policy explains how they are written, reviewed and corrected. The disclaimer explains their limits: they are educational, not accounting or legal advice.

Advertising

The public guide pages may show advertising served by Google AdSense. Advertising is always labelled and kept separate from editorial content, and it never influences what the guides say. The app's signed-in screens do not carry advertising. The cookie policy lists the advertising cookies Google may set and how to control them.

Privacy at a glance

  • No account is needed to read the guides.
  • The app does not charge you and does not take payment card or billing details.
  • Business records are yours; you can export or delete them at any time.
  • The public guides load advertising on content pages; the policy pages do not.
