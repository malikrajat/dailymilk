Contact

Contact Daily milk

Daily milk is run by one person, Rajat Malik. Email is the fastest way to reach me, and it is the only channel I monitor.

Email

contact@rajatmalik.dev

The app also has a contact form at /contact inside the application, which reaches the same mailbox. The form needs JavaScript and is protected by a captcha; email needs neither.

What to write about

  • Support. Something in the app is not working, or you are unsure how to record a particular case. Include the device and browser, and what you expected to happen.
  • Data requests. Access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your account data. Write from the email address on the account so I can verify the request.
  • Corrections. A guide is wrong, out of date, or unclear. Point to the page and the sentence; corrections are published with the date they were made.
  • Questions about a guide. If a question is likely to help other readers, it may be added to the FAQ.
  • Advertising and partnerships. The site does not sell sponsored posts or accept paid links. Advertising on the public guides is served by Google AdSense; enquiries about advertising on Google's network should go to Google.
  • Security. If you believe you have found a vulnerability, email first and do not publish it. Include enough detail to reproduce the issue.

What to expect

I read every message. Replies usually take a few days, and longer around holidays. Support is provided on a best-effort basis: Daily milk is free and is not a commercial service with a support contract.

Data-deletion and data-access requests are actioned as soon as the request is verified. See the privacy policy for what is stored and how deletion works.

Who runs this site

Daily milk is an independent personal project run by Rajat Malik. It is not a company, and there is no support team behind the address. Read about the app for the full description, or the editorial policy for how the guides are written.

Please do not send sensitive business data

Email is not a secure vault. Do not send customer lists, payment details or exported database files by email. For anything that needs account-specific records, describe the problem first and I will tell you what is needed.
