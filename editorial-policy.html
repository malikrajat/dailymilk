Editorial policy

This page explains who writes the guides, how they are checked, how corrections work, and how advertising is kept away from editorial decisions.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who writes the guides

The guides are written and maintained by Rajat Malik, who also builds and runs Daily milk. They are based on how small milk delivery businesses actually keep their records — the fields a round produces, the ways bills are calculated, and the points where paper, spreadsheets and software each fail.

There is no separate newsroom. That is a limitation as well as a simplification: every page is one person's work, and every page names that person and the date it was last changed.

What the guides are for

Every guide has to help someone do a job: record a delivery, calculate a bill, plan a route, or find where milk is being lost. Content that does not serve a task — commentary, filler, keyword pages — is not published.

The guides are educational. They are not accounting, tax, legal or financial advice, and they do not promise a business outcome. Read the disclaimer for the full statement.

Content the site does not publish

The site does not publish, and does not accept advertising against, content in Google's restricted categories: sexually explicit content, shocking or graphic violence, explosives, firearms and other weapons, tobacco, recreational drugs, alcohol misuse, online gambling, prescription-drug sales, or unapproved pharmaceuticals and supplements.

It also does not publish hateful or harassing material, content that enables dishonest behaviour, deceptive or manipulated media, or material that infringes copyright or other intellectual property. Milk delivery record keeping is the entire subject area.

How content is researched and checked

  • Worked examples are calculated, not estimated. Every arithmetic example on this site is checked line by line so that the totals a reader verifies by hand agree with the page.
  • Local rules are not assumed. Where a rate structure or tax rule varies by country or by dairy, the page says so instead of presenting one practice as universal.
  • Software claims are verifiable. Statements about what Daily milk stores, exports or sends are checked against the app and the privacy policy, and the policy is the authoritative version if the two ever differ.
  • Dates are real. The "last updated" date changes when the text changes, not when the site is rebuilt.

Corrections

If a guide is wrong, it is corrected and the correction is noted. To report an error, email contact@rajatmalik.dev with the page URL and the sentence that is wrong. Substantive corrections are reflected in the page's "last updated" date; a correction that changes a calculation is explained in the text rather than silently applied.

Advertising and independence

The public guide articles may display advertising served by Google AdSense. Advertising is what funds the hosting and the time spent writing and maintaining the pages.

  • Ads are labelled and are visually separated from editorial content. They are never styled to look like part of an article, and they are never placed under a misleading heading such as "resources" or "helpful links". Only standard advertising labels are used.
  • Advertisers do not see articles before publication and cannot request changes to editorial content.
  • The site does not sell sponsored posts, paid links or rankings. No one can pay to have a product recommended in a guide.
  • Where the site's own app is mentioned, that is disclosed in the article, as it is in the record-system comparison.
  • Policy pages carry no advertising. The privacy policy, terms, cookie policy, disclaimer and this page are free of ad units.

AI assistance

Drafting and editing tools may be used to help structure a page or check arithmetic. The operator remains responsible for every published sentence, reviews the content before it goes live, and corrects errors reported by readers. Pages are not published to fill a quota; they are published when they are accurate enough to help.

Reader privacy

Reading a guide does not require an account. The static pages load one small local stylesheet and, on content pages, the advertising script. What that script and Google's advertising partners do with cookies is described in the cookie policy and the privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy, a correction, or a complaint about an article: contact@rajatmalik.dev. See also the contact page.
