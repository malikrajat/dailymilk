Privacy Policy

Daily milk is a free personal project. I do not charge you, and I do not take payment card or billing details. This policy explains what is stored to run the app, what advertising on the public guides does, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version

  • Reading the guides needs no account. The app needs a free account.
  • I do not sell your personal information, and I do not use your business records for advertising or profiling.
  • The public guide pages may show advertising from Google AdSense, which uses cookies as described in section 5.
  • You can export or delete your data at any time, and you can change your advertising-cookie choices.

1. Who is responsible

Daily milk is an application for recording and reporting on a milk delivery business: milkmen, daily milk entries, milk rates and payments. It is an independent personal project run by Rajat Malik, an individual developer. It is not a company, and there is no business entity behind it.

For the purposes of data protection law, the operator of this site is the controller of the personal information described below. Contact: contact@rajatmalik.dev.

2. What is stored to run the app

The app cannot work without some information. This is the complete list, and it exists to provide the service to you.

2.1 Account information

When you create an account or sign in with Google, I receive your email address, your display name and a unique account identifier. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. I never receive or store your Google password.

2.2 Business records you enter

The information you record in the app — milkman details, daily milk entries, milk rates, payments and your application settings — is stored in your account so it is available on every device you sign in from. It is your data, not mine. I do not read it for my own purposes and do not use it to build advertising profiles.

2.3 Security and device information

To help you review account activity, the app records sign-in sessions and trusted devices, including a timestamp, the browser's user agent string, and the IP address and approximate geographic location reported for that connection. The approximate location is obtained by sending the request's IP address to the third-party service ipwho.is (see section 7).

2.4 Preferences

Formatting choices such as your currency and colour theme are stored with your settings so the app displays consistently on every device.

2.5 Contact form

The name, email address, optional phone number and message you enter on the contact page are used only to reply to you. They are not added to a mailing list and are not used for marketing. The form is delivered to my inbox by Web3Forms and protected by an hCaptcha check (see section 7). I keep nothing beyond the reply I send you.

3. Reading the public pages

The guides, about page, contact page and policy pages are static HTML. They do not require an account and do not set a first-party cookie. They load a stylesheet from this site and, on content pages only, the Google AdSense script described in section 5.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The app uses browser storage for functional purposes: keeping you signed in, remembering your theme and preferences, and storing the app's files and queued offline entries so it works without a connection. That storage is not used for advertising.

The public guide pages additionally load Google advertising, which may set cookies. The cookie policy lists the storage used, the advertising cookies involved, and how to control them.

5. Advertising on the public guides

Advertising is served by Google AdSense only on the individual guide articles that contain substantial editorial content. It is not used on the app's signed-in screens, the guides index, the about page, the contact page or any policy page. Advertising is labelled and kept separate from editorial content, and it never influences what a guide says.

5.1 Google advertising cookies

Google uses cookies to help serve the ads it displays on this site. When you visit a page that carries advertising, a cookie may be placed on your browser. The required disclosures are:

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

You can also opt out through youronlinechoices.eu (Europe) or optout.networkadvertising.org (United States).

5.2 Third-party vendors and ad networks

The third-party vendor serving advertising on this site is Google (Google AdSense and Google's advertising partners). Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy, and Google publishes the list of ad technology providers it permits. Those vendors may place and read cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and other identifiers on your browser, or use them to collect information as a result of ad serving on this site.

For how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services (also available at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

5.3 Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and Brazil)

Where the law requires consent for advertising cookies — in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Brazil — Google's certified consent management message is shown and records your choice before those cookies are set. This supports compliance with the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the LGPD and Google's EU user consent policy. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the cookie controls.

5.4 What this site does not do

  • It does not set cookies on Google's domains, and does not modify, intercept or delete cookies set on Google's domains.
  • It does not pass personally identifiable information to Google for advertising. The contact form is delivered to the operator's inbox and is not sent to Google.
  • It does not collect precise device or GPS location for advertising. The only location data is the approximate city, region and country derived from a sign-in IP address for the security activity list (section 2.3).
  • It does not offer money, rewards or other compensation for viewing or clicking ads, and it does not ask visitors to click ads.
  • It does not use interest-based advertising to target children, and the site is not directed to children (section 13).
  • It does not sell personal information for money. Some advertising cookies may be treated as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under certain US state privacy laws; the opt-out links above and the consent message let you limit that use.

6. How information is used

  • To operate the app and display your records and reports.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To show you recent account and security activity so unusual sign-ins are visible.
  • To export your database as JSON, and to import it on request.
  • To respond to your support requests and corrections.
  • To serve and measure advertising on the public guides, subject to your consent where required.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not use your business records to train advertising or profiling systems.

7. Service providers

I rely on the following third parties. Each processes data only as needed to provide its service, and none of them receives payment from you:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) — hosts your account and your business records.
  • Google AdSense — serves advertising on the public guide pages and may set advertising cookies as described in section 5.
  • ipwho.is — receives the IP address of a sign-in request in order to return an approximate city, region and country for your security activity list.
  • Web3Forms — receives the contact form fields and forwards them to my email inbox.
  • hCaptcha — checks the contact form for automated submissions. It is used only on the contact page.

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Firebase encrypts data in transit and at rest according to Google's own security documentation.

8. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Brazil)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation, the UK GDPR or the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) applies, the legal bases are:

  • Performance of a contract — storing your account and business records so the app can work for you.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, and responding to support requests.
  • Consent — advertising cookies where consent is required, and any optional communication you ask for. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — responding to lawful requests where applicable.

9. Retention and deletion

Your records are kept for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes your records from Daily milk. Copies of exported files that you saved to your own device remain under your control and must be deleted by you. Contact-form messages are kept only as long as needed to deal with the enquiry.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Export. You can export your full database to CSV, PDF or JSON at any time, and import a JSON export on another device.
  • Access, correction and deletion. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data by writing to me from the address on the account.
  • Objection and restriction. Where processing relies on legitimate interests, you can object or ask for processing to be restricted.
  • Portability. The JSON export is a portable copy of your records in an open format.
  • Advertising choices. You can change or withdraw consent using the cookie controls, and you can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • Complaint. You can complain to a data protection authority in your country, including the national authority in Brazil (ANPD) where the LGPD applies. Asking me first is usually faster.

I respond to verified requests within the time required by applicable law. I may need to confirm that you control the account before acting on a deletion or access request.

11. International transfers

The service providers above are global providers and may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers rely on the safeguards those providers have published, such as standard contractual clauses. The providers' own privacy documentation describes their arrangements.

12. Security

Access to your records is controlled by your account. The app is served over HTTPS and uses security headers, including a Content Security Policy. No system is perfectly secure: choose a strong password, keep your sign-in credentials private, and export your data regularly so you have your own copy.

13. Children

Daily milk is a business tool and is not directed to children. It is not tagged as child-directed, and I do not knowingly collect information from children. Interest-based advertising is not used to target users known to be under 13 or content directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact me and I will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy as the app or the site changes. Material changes are reflected in the "last updated" date above. If advertising arrangements change in a way that affects your choices, this page and the cookie policy change together.

15. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data: contact@rajatmalik.dev. The contact page explains what to include.

This policy is also available in the app at /privacy. If the two versions ever differ, this static page is the authoritative version.
